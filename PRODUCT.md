# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Students (Grade 7 and above) from schools across the region — both first-time MUN delegates and experienced participants. Faculty advisors who register delegations. Parents who want to understand the conference. Primarily mobile-first Indian teenagers discovering the site through Instagram or WhatsApp links, but also desktop users doing serious research before committing.

Secondary audience: the delegates themselves during the conference — checking schedules, committee briefs, and resources on their phones.

## Product Purpose

The official conference website for Legatio 4.0, the fourth edition of DPS Siliguri's flagship Model United Nations (31 July – 2 August 2026). Theme: *Kurukshetra of Diplomacy — War & Peace*.

The site is the primary digital identity and information hub for the conference. It must feel like the conference itself — a destination, not a flyer. It handles everything from first impression through registration to delegate preparation (Resources, FAQ, Committee briefs, Schedule, Venue). Individual delegate registration lives here.

Success looks like: a prospective delegate opens the site, feels the weight and prestige of the conference immediately, and knows exactly what to do next.

## Brand Personality

Epic · Ancient · Diplomatic.

The conference theme is Kurukshetra — the Mahabharata battlefield, the ultimate war-and-peace crucible. The brand draws from aged palm-leaf manuscripts, war-room briefings, ink and parchment, saffron and blood maroon, brass gold and lapis. Ancient Bharat meets modern diplomacy. The tone is grave, ceremonial, and charged with real stakes — not playful, not corporate. Typography leans into Roman gravitas (Cinzel) and letterpress warmth (EB Garamond). Every element should feel like it belongs in a folio, not a pitch deck.

## Anti-references

- **Generic UN-blue MUN sites** — navy blue, formal UN seals, committee-roster spreadsheet energy. The aesthetic of "we have a conference" not "we are the conference."
- **Startup / SaaS landing pages** — gradient hero, metric cards, Geist/Inter, parchment-cream AI defaults, "✓ Built for teams" copy. Zero brand specificity.
- **Generic school event sites** — amateur layout, clip art, Comic Sans energy, colour-by-numbers.
- **Over-produced conference sites** — neon, particle effects for their own sake, animations that distract rather than honour. Motion here should feel like a slow ritual, not a product demo.

## Design Principles

1. **The folio, not the flyer.** Every section should feel like a page of an ancient manuscript — considered, weighty, permanent. Information is revealed, not dumped.
2. **Honour the theme.** Kurukshetra is not a metaphor to be mentioned once. War and peace, Arjuna and the chariot, Chanakya and the state — these should be felt in the texture of the interface.
3. **Stakes over spectacle.** Motion and colour exist to communicate gravity, not to impress. A slow ink-reveal carries more weight than a spinning particle field.
4. **Prestige through restraint.** The most prestigious institutions don't shout. White space, a heavy serif, a single accent — these signal confidence. Clutter signals insecurity.
5. **Mobile delegates first.** Most users arrive via a phone link. Every interaction — registration, schedule, committee brief — must be frictionless at 375px before it's polished at 1440px.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA as the floor. Body text contrast ≥ 4.5:1 against all backgrounds. The parchment palette requires vigilance — muted text on warm-tinted backgrounds is the most common failure mode. All animations must respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Forms (registration) must be fully keyboard-navigable and screen-reader friendly.
